Privacy Policy

Ameli Ai (“Ameli,” “we,” “us,” or “our”)
Effective date: October 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and share information when you use the Ameli Ai mobile application and related services (the “Services”). Ameli is a wellness and lifestyle app that offers therapy-informed AI conversation, journaling, mood tracking, mindfulness tools, and optional health data integrations. Ameli is not a medical provider and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

1. Information we collect

Depending on how you use the Services, we may collect:

  • Contact info: name and email address when you create an account (including Google sign-in).
  • Identifiers: account / user ID associated with your Ameli account.
  • User content: journal entries, mood logs, chat messages with Ameli AI, gratitude notes, profile photo, and voice recordings you choose to create (for example mindful-minute voice notes).
  • Health & fitness data: information you enter in assessments or wellness features, and—if you grant permission—data from Apple HealthKit / Google Health Connect such as heart rate, blood pressure, steps, or related activity metrics.
  • Usage data: information about how you interact with the app (for example feature use and session activity) needed to operate and improve the Services.
  • Technical data: basic device and app information needed for security, authentication (including optional Face ID / device biometrics stored on your device), and reliable delivery of the Services. We do not use the advertising identifier for ads.

We do not require a physical address, phone contacts list, or precise GPS location to use Ameli.

2. How we use information

We use the information above to:

  • Create and manage your account and authenticate you
  • Provide app features (AI chat, journaling, mood insights, mindfulness, health displays)
  • Personalize wellness-related experiences within the app
  • Maintain security, prevent fraud/abuse, and troubleshoot issues
  • Communicate about the Services (for example account or product notices)
  • Comply with law and enforce our Terms of Service

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use your data for third-party advertising or cross-app tracking as defined by Apple.

3. Health data

If you enable HealthKit or Health Connect, health and fitness data is used only to provide the wellness features you request (for example displaying metrics or related insights). We do not use HealthKit data for advertising or sell it. You can revoke health permissions in your device settings at any time.

4. AI conversations

Messages you send to Ameli AI and related outputs are processed to provide the chat experience. AI responses may be inaccurate or incomplete and are for wellness support only—not clinical care. Do not use Ameli for emergencies. If you are in crisis in the U.S., call or text 988, or call local emergency services.

5. Sharing with service providers

We share data only as needed to operate the Services, for example:

  • Cloud / backend providers that host accounts, databases, and storage (for example authentication and content you save)
  • AI / infrastructure providers that process chat inputs to generate responses
  • Platform providers such as Apple or Google for sign-in, push notifications, or app distribution

These providers are only permitted to process data to perform services for us and must protect it appropriately. We may also disclose information if required by law, to protect users or the Services, or in connection with a merger or sale of assets.

6. Data retention

We keep your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the Services, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements. You may request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting us (see Contact).

7. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure; please use a strong unique password and protect your device.

8. Children’s privacy

The Services are not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information, contact us and we will take appropriate steps to delete it.

9. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or export your personal information, or to object to certain processing. You can update profile information in the app, revoke device permissions (microphone, photos, health, Face ID) in system settings, and request account deletion by emailing us. We will respond in accordance with applicable law.

10. International transfers

We may process information in the United States and other countries where we or our providers operate. By using the Services, you understand your information may be transferred to and processed in those locations.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the updated version here and update the effective date. Continued use of the Services after changes means you accept the updated policy.
