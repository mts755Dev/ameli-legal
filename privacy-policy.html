Ameli (“we,” “us,” or “our”) is a wellness and lifestyle app with therapy-informed AI conversation, journaling, mood tracking, mindfulness tools, and optional health integrations. Ameli is not a medical provider and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

1. Information we collect

Depending on how you use the Services, we may collect:

  • Contact info: name and email address when you create an account (including Google sign-in).
  • Identifiers: account / user ID associated with your Ameli account.
  • User content: journal entries, mood logs, chat messages with Ameli, gratitude notes, profile photo, and voice recordings you choose to create.
  • Health & fitness data: assessment answers and—if you grant permission—HealthKit / Health Connect metrics such as heart rate, blood pressure, or steps.
  • Usage data: how you interact with the app, needed to operate and improve the Services.
  • Technical data: basic device and app information for security and delivery. Optional Face ID stays on your device. We do not use the advertising identifier for ads.

We do not require a physical address, phone contacts list, or precise GPS location to use Ameli.

2. How we use information

  • Create and manage your account and authenticate you
  • Provide app features (AI chat, journaling, mood insights, mindfulness, health displays)
  • Personalize wellness-related experiences within the app
  • Maintain security, prevent fraud/abuse, and troubleshoot issues
  • Communicate about the Services (account or product notices)
  • Comply with law and enforce our Terms of Service

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use your data for third-party advertising or cross-app tracking as defined by Apple.

3. Health data

If you enable HealthKit or Health Connect, health and fitness data is used only to provide the wellness features you request. We do not use HealthKit data for advertising or sell it. You can revoke health permissions in your device settings at any time.

4. AI conversations

Messages you send to Ameli and related outputs are processed to provide the chat experience. AI responses may be inaccurate or incomplete and are for wellness support only—not clinical care. Do not use Ameli for emergencies. If you are in crisis in the U.S., call or text 988, or call local emergency services.

5. Sharing with service providers

We share data only as needed to operate the Services, for example:

  • Cloud / backend providers that host accounts, databases, and storage
  • AI / infrastructure providers that process chat inputs to generate responses
  • Platform providers such as Apple or Google for sign-in, push notifications, or app distribution

These providers may only process data to perform services for us. We may also disclose information if required by law, to protect users or the Services, or in connection with a merger or sale of assets.

6. Data retention

We keep your information while your account is active or as needed to provide the Services, comply with law, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements. Request deletion by contacting us below.

7. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure—use a strong unique password and protect your device.

8. Children’s privacy

The Services are not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Contact us if you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information.

9. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or export your personal information, or object to certain processing. Update profile info in the app, revoke device permissions in system settings, and request account deletion through the Ameli app. We will respond in accordance with applicable law.

10. International transfers

We may process information in the United States and other countries where we or our providers operate. By using the Services, you understand your information may be transferred to and processed in those locations.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the updated version here and update the effective date. Continued use after changes means you accept the updated policy.
