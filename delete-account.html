Delete your Ameli account

How to request deletion of your Ameli account and the data tied to it.

This page is for users of the Ameli mobile app. You can request that your account and associated personal data be deleted at any time.

1. How to request account deletion

Use one of these methods:

  • In the Ameli app (preferred): Open Ameli → go to Profile → look for account or settings options related to deleting your account, or contact support from within the app and ask to delete your account. Include the email address used to sign up.
  • By email: Send a message to support@ameli.app with the subject line “Delete my Ameli account”. Include:
    • The email address on your Ameli account
    • A clear statement that you want your account and associated data deleted

We may ask you to confirm the request from your registered email so we can verify that you own the account.

2. Data we delete

When your deletion request is completed, we delete or irreversibly anonymize data associated with your account, including where applicable:

  • Account profile information (such as name and email)
  • User ID / account identifiers
  • Journal entries, mood logs, gratitude notes, and similar content you created
  • Chat / AI conversation history tied to your account
  • Voice notes and profile photos you uploaded
  • Health and fitness data stored in Ameli in connection with your account (device Health data on your phone is controlled by you in system settings)

3. Data we may keep

We may retain limited information when we are required to do so by law, or when needed for security, fraud prevention, or dispute resolution. Examples can include:

  • Records needed to comply with legal or regulatory obligations
  • Limited logs needed to detect abuse or secure our systems
  • Information already anonymized so it can no longer identify you

Any retained data is kept only as long as reasonably necessary for those purposes, then deleted or anonymized.

4. How long it takes

We aim to process account deletion requests within 30 days of verifying your request. Some backup systems may take a short additional period to fully clear residual copies, after which the data is no longer recoverable for normal use.
